# Information Security Guidelines

## Password Policy
Minimum 12 characters combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Cannot reuse last 5 passwords. Must change every 90 days. Account locks after 5 failed login attempts. Multi-factor authentication required for all systems.

## Data Classification
Public: No restrictions on distribution. Internal: Company employees only. Confidential: Need-to-know basis with manager approval. Restricted: Executive approval required, legal review needed.

## Access Control
Principle of least privilege enforced. Role-based access control implemented across all systems. Access reviews conducted quarterly. Terminated employee access revoked within 1 hour.

## Email and Communication
Do not open suspicious attachments. Verify sender before clicking links. Use encrypted email for confidential information. BCC external recipients when sending to groups. Report phishing attempts to security team.

## Device Security
Enable full disk encryption on all devices. Install security updates within 48 hours. Use company-approved antivirus software. Never leave devices unattended in public. Report lost or stolen devices immediately.

## Incident Response
Report security incidents within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate on your own. Preserve evidence by not touching affected systems. Follow incident response team instructions. Complete incident report within 24 hours.

## Compliance Requirements
Complete annual security training by deadline. Acknowledge security policy updates within 5 days. Report policy violations without fear of retaliation. Understand that violations may result in termination.
