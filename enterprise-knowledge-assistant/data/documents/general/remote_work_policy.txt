# Remote Work Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

## Eligibility
Employees must complete a 6-month probationary period and receive manager approval. Role must be suitable for remote work with demonstrated ability to work independently.

## Work Schedule
Standard business hours apply (9 AM - 5 PM local time). Core collaboration hours are 10 AM - 3 PM when all team members must be available. Flexible scheduling available with manager approval.

## Equipment and Technology
Company provides laptop, monitor, and necessary peripherals. Employees responsible for reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps). VPN required for all work activities. IT support available during business hours.

## Workspace Requirements
Dedicated workspace free from distractions required. Ergonomic setup recommended to prevent injury. Workspace must meet basic safety standards. Background appropriate for video calls.

## Communication Expectations
Respond to messages within 2 hours during work hours. Attend all scheduled meetings with camera on. Update Slack status to reflect availability. Weekly check-ins with direct manager mandatory.

## Security and Confidentiality
Use company VPN for all work activities. Lock computer when away from desk. Never use public WiFi for confidential work. Follow data protection policies strictly. Report security incidents immediately.

## Performance Management
Same performance standards as office workers. Regular performance reviews conducted. Productivity measured by outcomes, not hours. Right to return to office can be revoked if performance declines.

## Expenses
Monthly internet stipend: $50. Phone reimbursement: $40 per month. Office supplies available upon request. Co-working space requires pre-approval.
